Flare Fireplace Quotes v1.6.8

WHAT CHANGED
- Each fireplace configuration now has a quantity selector for projects using multiple identical fireplaces.
- Base fireplace pricing, selected-feature pricing, media quantities, quote totals, preview rows, and email wording all reflect the selected quantity.
- Passive Heat Flex selections now use the exact Passive Heat Flex framing guide for the selected model and glass height while excluding standard Free Flow wood and metal framing guides.
- Generated PDF filenames now end with the customer's name.
- Quote PDFs show Qty and Total MSRP columns for both the fireplace and optional features.

VALIDATION
- Automated regression tests cover quantity pricing and persistence, the full Passive Heat Flex guide matrix, and filename behavior.
- A representative three-fireplace PDF was rendered and visually inspected for table layout, quantities, totals, filename, and hyperlinks.

- 1.6.8
